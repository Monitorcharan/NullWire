First-Time Installation Guide (Windows SmartScreen)

Windows Defender shows a standard "Windows protected your PC — Unknown Publisher" notice on newly published open-source tools that do not pay for $400/year corporate certificates. NullWire is 100% clean and virus-free.

Step 1 Click "More info"

On the blue SmartScreen popup window, click the underlined More info link.

Step 2 Click "Run anyway"

Click the Run anyway button at the bottom right to start the installation.

Android Allow APK Source

When opening NullWireReceiver.apk, toggle "Allow from this source" if prompted.
