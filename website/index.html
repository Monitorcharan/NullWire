Step-by-Step Guide

How to Use NullWire & Configure Audio

Follow these 4 simple steps to route your Windows audio to your phone with zero hassle.

Step 1

Configure Windows Sound Output

Click the Sound icon on your Windows taskbar (bottom right) and select your active playback device (e.g. Realtek Speakers, Headphones, or Virtual Audio Cable).

💡 Silent PC Tip: To hear sound only on your phone, keep your default speaker active in Windows and simply plug in an empty 3.5mm jack or route through a Virtual Audio Cable.
Step 2

Connect on Wi-Fi or USB Tethering

Ensure your PC and Android phone are on the same Wi-Fi network (5GHz Wi-Fi is recommended for sub-3ms speed).

⚡ Zero Latency Mode: Enable USB Tethering on your Android phone for ultra-low < 2ms tournament wired audio.
Step 3

Launch & Start Streaming

Open NullWire on Windows. Select your audio device from the dropdown, click your discovered Android phone in the list, and click "Start Streaming".

🎧 Audio immediately begins playing in real-time through your phone's earphones or speakers.
Step 4

Use Reverse Phone Microphone

In NullWire Pro on Android, tap "Start Phone Mic". On your PC, click "Receive Phone Mic".

🎙️ In Discord, OBS, or games, select the routed microphone device to talk with crystal clear voice!

How to Configure Windows Audio & Virtual Speakers

NullWire uses direct Windows WASAPI loopback to capture high-definition stereo audio from any active audio device on your PC. Here is how to configure your sound routing:

1

Select Playback Device

Click the Sound icon on your Windows taskbar. Choose your primary sound output (e.g. Realtek Audio, Headphones, or VB-Audio Virtual Cable).

2

Select Device in NullWire

Open NullWire Pro. In the Audio Source dropdown, choose the same playback device to capture game & music sound.

3

Silent Physical Speakers

To stream exclusively to your phone without PC speaker sound, switch Windows output to a virtual device (or plug dummy earphones into PC).

Discord & Game Routing: In Discord / OBS, set your input device to your microphone and output to your Windows default device. NullWire automatically handles low-latency synchronization!
