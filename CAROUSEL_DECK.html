The Problem

Why does wireless audio always lag or glitch?

Bluetooth A2DP Buffering Delay
Standard Bluetooth stacks introduce 150ms–250ms of latency, making competitive gaming and movies unwatchable without lip-sync lag.
Asynchronous Hardware Clock Drift
PC audio DACs (48,000.1 Hz) and mobile DACs (47,998.9 Hz) run on separate crystals, causing buffer starvation clicks ("chirp chirp").
Heavy Web Runtime Companions
Existing PC apps bundle Chromium/Electron eating 250MB+ RAM for simple audio streaming.
