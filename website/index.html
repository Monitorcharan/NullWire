Installing NullWire on Android (Direct APK Sideload)

Because NullWire is distributed directly as an APK without Google Play account lock-in, Android displays a standard prompt asking for permission to install apps from your browser or file manager.

1

Download APK

Download NullWireReceiver.apk on your Android device from the download section.

2

Allow Unknown Source

When prompted by Android, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

3

Install & Stream

Tap Install. Launch NullWire, connect to your Wi-Fi, and enjoy sub-3ms audio!

Privacy Verification: NullWire requires 0 root permissions, 0 location access, 0 camera access, and 0 contacts access. It only utilizes local network UDP sockets and standard microphone audio capture for the reverse mic.
